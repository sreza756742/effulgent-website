# 1. Strategic Overview

## Business Concept

Effulgent provides personalised gift shopping, gift wrapping, occasion planning, delivery coordination, and corporate gifting.

The business removes the stress of choosing, buying, presenting, and delivering thoughtful gifts.

## Core Value Proposition

> Effulgent takes care of the planning, shopping, wrapping, and delivery so customers can give beautifully without the stress.

## Market Positioning

Effulgent should be positioned as a warm, premium, personal gifting concierge rather than a standard gift shop.

The business competes through:

- Personalised recommendations
- Convenience
- Professional presentation
- Local sourcing
- Flexible service packages
- Last-minute support
- Corporate customisation

## Primary Business Goal

Generate qualified enquiries and bookings through WhatsApp.

## Secondary Business Goals

- Present Effulgent as trustworthy and professional
- Explain services clearly
- Display transparent service pricing
- Generate corporate gifting enquiries
- Capture quote requests
- Build repeat and seasonal customers

2. Keep WhatsApp visible throughout the website.
3. Make pricing easy to understand.
4. Explain that gift costs are separate from service fees.
5. Reduce uncertainty with FAQs and clear service-area information.
6. Use personal language for individual customers.
7. Use professional language for corporate customers.
8. Make the corporate offer feel limited and time-sensitive without creating false urgency.

## Calculator Step 2: How Many Gifts?

### Controls

- Minus button
- Quantity field
- Plus button

### Default Quantity

> 10 gifts

### Pricing Logic

- 5–9 gifts: NZ$55 per gift
- 10–19 gifts: NZ$45 per gift
- 20+ gifts: NZ$38 per gift

### Calculator Rules

```text
if quantity >= 5 and quantity <= 9:
    price_per_gift = 55
elif quantity >= 10 and quantity <= 19:
    price_per_gift = 45
elif quantity >= 20:
    price_per_gift = 38
else:
    show_message("Minimum order is 5 gifts")
```

The final production implementation must validate this logic server-side or through a protected configuration layer if quotes are stored or transmitted.
